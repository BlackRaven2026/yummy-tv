YUMMY – TV MENU  (3 x MAX 43MT304S, Android 11)
========================================================

This folder IS the website. Put it online once (GitHub Pages, free), point
each TV at its page, and from then on you update the menu from home.

  Page for each TV                Live (animated)     Video (for slow TVs)
  TV 1 Palačinke & Specijaliteti  tv1.html            tv1-video.html
  TV 2 Mini palačinke i krofnice  tv2.html            tv2-video.html
  TV 3 Napravi svoju palačinku    tv3.html            tv3-video.html
  Start page (pick a TV)          index.html

Santa flies non-stop in spiral loops from TV 1 -> TV 2 -> TV 3 and back to
TV 1 (one lap = 30 s), tossing little party hats and gifts. Put the TVs side
by side in order 1 - 2 - 3. Both the live and the video pages are timed by
the clock, so the 3 TVs stay in step by themselves (even if one TV uses the
live page and another the video page).

STEP 2 – TEST ON ONE TV FIRST
-----------------------------
Open https://blackraven2026.github.io/yummy-tv/ in the TV's browser, choose
"Live" for that TV and watch for a minute.
  - Snow, lights and Santa move smoothly  -> use the Live pages (tvX.html).
  - Choppy / stuttering                   -> use the Video pages (tvX-video.html).


STEP 3 – SET UP EACH TV (once per TV)
-------------------------------------
1. TV settings -> Date & time -> "Automatic" (internet time). This is what
   keeps the 3 TVs in step.
2. TV settings -> turn OFF sleep timer, auto power off, "no signal" standby
   and eco / power-saving standby.
3. Install "Fully Kiosk Browser" from the TV's app store (free; you do NOT
   need the PLUS licence – just leave PLUS features switched off).
4. Open Fully Kiosk. In its settings:
     Start URL            -> https://blackraven2026.github.io/yummy-tv/
                             (the SAME short address on all 3 TVs)
     Launch on boot       -> ON (the menu starts when the TV turns on)
     Keep screen on       -> ON
     Fullscreen / hide status & navigation bar -> ON
     Web content: Autoplay videos -> ON (needed for the video pages)
   The start page opens: pick this TV's "Live" button with the remote
   (TV 1, TV 2 or TV 3). The TV remembers it. From now on, when it turns on,
   it shows "Otvaram TV 2 (Live) za 8 s..." and opens its menu by itself.
   To pick again: press OK on "Promijeni izbor" during those 8 seconds.
   Typing tip: plug a USB keyboard into the TV, or use the phone keyboard
   in the Google TV app (same Wi-Fi) to type/paste the address.
5. Restart the TV once to check it starts straight into the menu.


STEP 4 – UPDATING FROM HOME  (Yummy TV Studio)
----------------------------------------------
Do NOT edit the files in this folder by hand any more – they are made by
the studio and get overwritten.

  1. Open the folder  Yummy Website \ tv-menu-studio  and double-click
     "START STUDIO.bat". The editor opens in your browser
     (http://localhost:5178). Keep the black window open while you work.
  2. Change prices, names, ingredients, pictures; add or remove items;
     hide sold-out items (eye button); pick a theme at the top
     (Nova godina, Uskrs, Yummy crni, Yummy bijeli).
     Click anything on the TV picture to jump straight to it.
     Everything saves by itself. Made a mistake?  ↶ Undo.
  3. Click "⬆ Publish to TVs" -> "Upload to TVs". Done.
  4. Only if some TV uses the VIDEO page: click "🎬 Videos" ->
     "Make new videos" (about 4 min per TV), then "Upload to TVs".

If uploading ever fails: open GitHub Desktop (signed in as BlackRaven2026),
File -> Add local repository -> choose this "tv-menu-site" folder,
then press "Push origin".

Good to know:
  - If the internet goes down, the TVs keep showing the menu they have.
  - Each TV also refreshes itself every morning at 06:00.
  - Updates can take up to ~15 minutes to appear (GitHub's cache).


BACKUP – WITHOUT INTERNET (USB)
-------------------------------
Copy video/Yummy-tv1.mp4 (tv2, tv3) to a USB stick, play it in the TV's
media player with "Repeat / Ponavljanje" ON. Each is a 60 s seamless loop.
(No remote updates and no syncing between TVs this way.)
